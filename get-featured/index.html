Questions owners ask

Is there a free option?

Yes. A basic listing on Oahu Fishing Guides is free for fishing charters and guides serving Oʻahu. We verify every operator first. Featured is an optional upgrade at $29 a month.

What exactly do I get with Featured?

A top spot in the charter and fishing-style sections on Oahu Fishing Guides, a Featured badge, one photo, a short pitch in your words and a direct link to your booking page. You can swap the photo or pitch whenever your season or offer changes.

Will Featured guarantee bookings?

No. We promise the placement and the work behind it, not a number of bookings. Visitors still decide based on your reviews, price, safety record and how easy you are to book.

How is a paid spot labeled?

Every paid placement shows a visible Featured badge, and the link to your site is marked rel="sponsored". That keeps the guide honest for readers and within search engine rules, and it protects your own site too.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Give 30 days’ written notice at eyetoad.com/cancel/ or info@eyetoad.com. Your spot stays live through the notice period and then returns to a basic listing; charges during the notice period still apply. All sales are final.
